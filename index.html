🎓 DropGuard — Student Dropout Risk Predictor

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A logistic regression model trained on 900 synthetic student records estimates dropout risk from attendance, previous GPA, assignment completion, backlogs, study hours, part-time work hours and fee stress. 5-fold CV: accuracy = 73.4%, AUC = 0.80 vs an always-continuing baseline of 61.1%.

How it works: the inputs are standardised with the training means and standard deviations, multiplied by the model's learned coefficients and passed through a sigmoid. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try a sample: an average student — 78% attendance, 7.2 GPA, 70% assignments, 1 backlog, 12 study hours → about 30.51% dropout risk. An at-risk one — 55% attendance, 5.0 GPA, 30% assignments, 4 backlogs, 3 study hours, 20 part-time hours, fee stress → about 99.83% dropout risk. A strong student — 95% attendance, 9.0 GPA, 95% assignments, no backlogs, 25 study hours → about 0.62% risk.

This is a classroom demo on synthetic data, to flag students who may need support early — not a real academic decision.
